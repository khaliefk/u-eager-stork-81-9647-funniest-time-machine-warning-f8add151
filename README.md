Entry for the [Funniest Time Machine Warning Label](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-time-machine-warning-f8add151) bounty on make.fun. One warning label, 120 characters at most, judged by an AI comedy judge in head-to-head rounds.

## The entry

> WARNING: Do not step on any butterflies. One of them is your grandfather, and he remembers.

91 characters. The joke turns the grandfather paradox into a safety notice: the hazard is real, the tone stays deadpan, and the punch lands in the last two words.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the label to `/output/entry.txt` and refuses to run if the text ever drifts outside the 1 to 120 character gate.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
